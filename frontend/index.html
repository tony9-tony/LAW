Support

Common questions, answered.

The questions people ask most. The full list, with the step-by-step guide, is on How it works.

How do I make a request?

Sign in (or create a free account) and press New request. A short 5-step form asks for the type of matter, what happened and how you want help. Your matter opens once the firm accepts it and your payment is confirmed.

How do payments work?

An invoice appears in your portal with a Pay now section. Pay with the Lipa Namba shown there, use the invoice number as the reference, and upload the receipt. The firm confirms it and the invoice shows Paid.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Call or WhatsApp the firm. They set a temporary password for you; sign in with it, then choose your own under Profile.

How do I contact the office?

Call or WhatsApp +255 714 840 951 or +255 657 259 584. The office is in Posta, Kisutu, Tanzania; visits are by appointment.
