Support

Common questions, answered.

Short answers about consultations, requests, payments, and contacting the office. For anything account-specific, sign in to the client portal — or ask the AI Support assistant at any time.

Who is ET CETRA?

ET CETRA ADVOCATES COMPANY LIMITED is a technology-led advocates’ chambers led by Emmanuel Richard Machibya, Advocate & Legal Counsel, based in Posta, Kisutu, Tanzania.

How do I book a consultation?

Use Book Consultation to enter the existing consultation flow. You will sign in (or create an account), pick a type and time, and submit — then track its status and payment from your portal.

How do I make a request?

Use Make a Request to open the guided 5-step matter intake. The firm reviews it before any formal matter is opened — a request is not an accepted matter until any required payment is completed and approved.

How do payments work?

When payment is required, an invoice appears in your portal with a Pay Now section. Follow the displayed method and instructions, upload your proof with a reference, and the firm reviews it — once approved, the invoice is marked as paid.

Where do I find the Lipa Number and QR code?

Inside your own invoice’s Pay Now section in the portal — always use exactly what is displayed there.

Do I need an account?

Chat and these answers are public. Booking, requesting, paying, messaging, and documents require signing in or creating a client account first.

How do I contact the office?

The office is in Posta, Kisutu, Tanzania (visits by appointment only). Confirmed telephone, email, and hours are published on the Contact page; until then the consultation or matter intake is the most reliable route.
