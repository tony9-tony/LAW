Step by step

How it works.

Everything happens in your private client portal: your request, the invoice, your payment, messages, documents and appointments. Here is exactly what to expect, from your first request to the service.

Before you start You need an email address and a password of at least 12 characters. Have any documents about your matter ready (photos or PDF are fine).

Path 1

From request to service.

Use this path when you need the firm to handle a matter: a dispute, a contract, a land issue, a family or employment matter.

  1. Create your account

    Go to Create account, enter your full name, email and a password, and sign in. Your client portal opens on the Dashboard.

    You

  2. Send a request

    In the portal press New request. Choose the type of matter, describe what happened in your own words, add the outcome you want and attach documents. Check the summary and press Submit. The request appears under My requests with the status Submitted.

    You

  3. The advocate reviews it

    The firm reads your request. If something is missing, the status changes to Action required and you will see what to add: reply from the request page. You receive a notification at every change.

    The firm

  4. Accepted: an invoice is issued

    When the firm accepts your request, an invoice appears under Invoices with the amount and a Pay now section. If the firm cannot take the matter, the request is marked Declined with a reason.

    The firm

  5. Pay and upload the receipt

    Pay the exact amount using the Lipa Namba shown on your invoice (any mobile network), and use the invoice number as the reference. Then upload a photo or PDF of the receipt with the transaction reference. The invoice shows Pending verification.

    You

  6. The firm confirms your payment

    The firm checks the payment. Once confirmed, the invoice is marked Paid and your matter is opened with its own reference number. If the receipt cannot be confirmed, it is marked Rejected with the reason, and you can upload it again.

    The firm

  7. Your matter is handled

    Open My matters to follow progress. Message the advocate, receive and upload Documents, and see your Appointments. The matter moves from Open to Active, then Resolved and Closed when the work is complete.

    You and the firm

What each status means

Submitted
Your request was received and is waiting for review.
Under review
The advocate is reading your request.
Action required
The firm needs more information from you. Open the request to reply.
Accepted
The firm will take the matter. Pay the invoice to start.
Declined
The firm cannot take the matter. The reason is shown.
Payment required
The invoice is waiting for your payment.
Pending verification
Your receipt was uploaded. The firm is checking it.
Paid
Payment confirmed. Your matter or appointment goes ahead.
Open / Active
Your matter is being worked on.
Resolved / Closed
The work is finished. Your documents stay available.
Path 2

Booking a consultation.

Use this path when you first want to talk to the advocate about your situation.

  1. Choose a time

    Sign in and open Book consultation. Choose the type of consultation, in person, phone or video, and a date and time. Add a short note and send it.

    You

  2. The firm confirms

    The firm confirms the booking or proposes another time. A confirmed consultation comes with its invoice under Invoices.

    The firm

  3. Pay before the meeting

    Pay with the Lipa Namba on the invoice and upload the receipt, exactly as for a request. Once the payment is confirmed, the appointment is final.

    You

  4. Meet the advocate

    Your appointment shows under Appointments with the date, time and place or call details. After the consultation you can send a full request if you want the firm to act for you.

    You and the firm

Getting started

Do I need an account?

Yes, to send a request, book a consultation, pay, message the firm or see documents. Creating an account is free: you only need your full name, an email address and a password of at least 12 characters. Reading this website and asking the AI assistant need no account.

What can the firm help me with?

Civil litigation and disputes, commercial matters and contracts, family law, property and land, and employment law. If your matter does not fit one of these, choose "Other" in the request form and describe it; the advocate will tell you whether the firm can help.

Should I send a request or book a consultation?

Send a request when you want the firm to handle a matter for you. Book a consultation when you first want to talk to the advocate about your situation. After a consultation you can still send a full request.

What should I prepare?

A short, plain description of what happened and what you want to achieve, and any documents you have: contracts, title deeds, letters, IDs or photos. Photos and PDF files are fine.

Requests and review

How long until the firm replies?

The advocate reviews requests in the order they arrive. You receive a notification in your portal at every change. If your matter is urgent, call or WhatsApp the firm after sending your request.

What does "Action required" mean?

The firm needs more information from you. Open the request in My requests to see what is missing and reply there. The review continues as soon as you answer.

Can the firm decline my request?

Yes. If the firm cannot take your matter, the request is marked Declined and the reason is shown to you. You are not charged for a declined request.

When does my matter start?

When the firm has accepted your request and confirmed your payment. Your matter then appears under My matters with its own reference number, for example M-ABC123.

Payments

How do I pay?

Open the invoice under Invoices and press Pay now. Pay the exact amount with the Lipa Namba shown there (any mobile network) and use the invoice number as the reference. Then upload a photo or PDF of the receipt with the transaction reference.

Where do I find the Lipa Namba?

Only inside your own invoice, in the Pay now section. Always use exactly what is shown there. The firm never asks you to pay to a person's number.

My receipt was rejected. What now?

The reason is shown on the invoice. Check it (for example the amount or the transaction reference), then upload the correct receipt again from the same invoice.

How do I know my payment was received?

After you upload the receipt the invoice shows Pending verification. When the firm confirms it, the invoice shows Paid and you get a notification. Your payments are listed under Payments.

Your matter

How do I talk to the advocate?

Use Messages or the conversation on your matter. On a new matter the firm writes first; you can reply as soon as they do.

How do I send or receive documents?

Open Documents. Upload what the firm asks for and download what the firm shares with you. Documents stay available after the matter is closed.

Where do I see my appointments?

Under Appointments, with the date, time and place or call details. Changes from the firm appear there and in Notifications.

Who can see my information?

Only you and the firm. Your requests, documents, invoices and messages are private to your account.

Account and password

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Call or WhatsApp the firm on +255 714 840 951 or +255 657 259 584 and give the email you registered with. The firm sets a temporary password and gives it to you directly. Sign in with it, then open Profile and choose your own under Change password. The portal does not send e-mails.

How do I change my password?

Sign in, open Profile, and use Change password. You need your current password and a new one of at least 12 characters.

How do I change my name or email?

Change your name under Profile. Your email is how you sign in, so to change it, send the firm a message and they will update it for you.

Why was I signed out?

For your security a sign-in lasts 8 hours. Sign in again with your email and password.
