# Information To Confirm

The following business information is intentionally not invented:

- Official law firm/company name and brand assets.
- Confirmed practice areas and service descriptions.
- Full professional biography, qualifications, registration details, and credentials.
- Public telephone, email, office hours, and address confirmation.
- Consultation policy, fees, availability, and intake questions.
- Privacy notice, terms, cookie policy, and data retention requirements.
- Staff accounts, roles, operational workflow, and document storage provider.
